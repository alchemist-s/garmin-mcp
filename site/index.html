What you can ask

Sleep

Stages, sleep score, overnight SpO2 and breathing rate.

Recovery

HRV against your baseline, Body Battery, stress, resting heart rate.

Activities

Pace, per-kilometre splits, heart rate, power, cadence, elevation.

Trends

Steps, intensity minutes and training load over any date range.

Three steps, once

  1. Download and open

    Claude Desktop recognises the file and shows an install dialog.

  2. Sign in to Garmin

    Your email and password. The password is used once, to get an access token that stays on your computer.

  3. Ask it something

    If you use two-step verification, Garmin emails a code — paste it into the chat. You won't be asked again for about a year.

Where your data goes

Straight to Garmin

Requests go from your computer to Garmin directly. There is no server in between, no account to create, and no analytics of any kind.

Your password is used once

It's exchanged for an access token stored on your machine, and never written to disk or logged by Zone Two.

It reads; it can't delete

Read-only unless you opt in at install. Even then it can only rename an activity or log a weight — no delete function exists in it.

Your questions reach Claude

Anything you ask about becomes part of your conversation, which Anthropic processes. That's how Claude works, and worth knowing with health data.

Worth knowing first

It uses Garmin's private app interface. Garmin's official API requires a commercial partner agreement, so Zone Two talks to the same interface the Garmin Connect app uses. It isn't supported by Garmin and could stop working if they change it.

What you get depends on your watch. Garmin only computes training readiness, training status and VO2 max on some models. On the others those come back empty — that's the device, not a fault.

It's one person's open-source project. Provided as-is, and best read as a way to talk to your own data rather than a product with a support desk.
